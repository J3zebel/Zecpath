Wearable Electronics Engineer

Job Overview

Designs compact, low-power electronics used in wearable devices like smartwatches, fitness
trackers, and medical sensors.
Responsibilities
• Design ultra-low-power electronic circuits
• Integrate biometric and health sensors
• Optimize battery life and device miniaturization
• Develop wireless connectivity systems (BLE, NFC)
• Ensure comfort, safety, and reliability
Skills Required
• Low-power electronics design
• Embedded systems programming
• Biomedical sensor basics
• Miniaturization techniques
