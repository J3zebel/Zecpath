Space Electronics Engineer

Job Overview
Develops high-reliability electronic systems used in satellites, spacecraft, and space
exploration missions.
Responsibilities
• Design radiation-hardened electronic circuits
• Develop satellite communication and control systems
• Ensure reliability in extreme space conditions
• Work on telemetry and tracking systems
• Test components for space qualification
Skills Required
• RF and communication systems
• High-reliability electronics design
• Space environment effects on electronics
• Aerospace systems fundamentals
