Electronics Design Engineer

Job Overview
Responsible for designing, developing, and testing electronic circuits and systems for
commercial or industrial applications.
Key Responsibilities
• Design electronic circuits for products and systems
• Develop schematics and system architecture
• Perform simulation and validation of designs
• Collaborate with PCB, embedded, and manufacturing teams
• Improve product performance and reliability
Required Skills
• Analog & digital circuit design
• Simulation tools (LTspice, Multisim)
• Strong electronics fundamentals
