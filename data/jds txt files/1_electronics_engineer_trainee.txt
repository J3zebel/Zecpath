Electronics Engineer (Trainee)

Job Overview
A trainee electronics engineer works under supervision to learn core electronics systems,
circuit design, testing, and basic troubleshooting in real-world projects.
Key Responsibilities
• Assist in electronic circuit assembly and testing
• Support senior engineers in design and development tasks
• Perform basic troubleshooting of electronic systems
• Study schematics and technical drawings
• Participate in lab experiments and prototype development
Required Skills
• Basic electronics (diodes, transistors, ICs)
• Circuit fundamentals
• Soldering and PCB basics
• Willingness to learn
