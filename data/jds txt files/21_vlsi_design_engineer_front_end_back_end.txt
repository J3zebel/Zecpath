VLSI Design Engineer (Front-End / Back-End)

Job Overview
Designs integrated circuits (ICs) at chip level.
Responsibilities
Front-End:
• RTL design using Verilog/VHDL
• Functional verification
• Simulation and debugging
Back-End:
• Physical design and layout
• Timing analysis
• Chip optimization
Skills Required
• HDL languages (Verilog/VHDL)
• Digital design concepts
• EDA tools (Cadence, Synopsys)
