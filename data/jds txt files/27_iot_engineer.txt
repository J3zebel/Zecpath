IoT Engineer

Job Overview
Builds smart connected devices that communicate over the internet.
Responsibilities
• Develop IoT-based embedded systems
• Integrate sensors and cloud platforms
• Implement wireless communication (WiFi, BLE, LoRa)
• Design IoT dashboards and data flow
• Ensure device security
Skills Required
• IoT protocols (MQTT, HTTP)
• Microcontrollers (ESP32, Arduino)
• Cloud platforms (AWS IoT, Azure IoT)
• Networking basics
