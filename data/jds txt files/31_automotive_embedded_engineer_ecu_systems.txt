Automotive Embedded Engineer (ECU Systems)

Job Overview
Designs embedded systems used in vehicles (Engine Control Units, infotainment, safety
systems).
Responsibilities
• Develop ECU software systems
• Work on CAN, LIN, and automotive protocols
• Ensure safety compliance (ISO 26262)
• Test automotive embedded systems
• Debug real-time vehicle systems
Skills Required
• Automotive communication protocols
• Embedded C programming
• CAN/LIN bus knowledge
• Safety standards understanding
