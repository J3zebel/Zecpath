RF Engineer (Radio Frequency Engineer)

Job Overview
RF Engineers design and develop circuits and systems that transmit and receive radio waves
used in wireless communication systems.
Key Responsibilities
• Design RF circuits such as amplifiers, filters, and oscillators
• Work on wireless communication systems (WiFi, LTE, 5G)
• Test RF performance using spectrum analyzers and network analyzers
• Reduce noise, interference, and signal loss
• Optimize antenna and transmitter/receiver performance
Required Skills
• Electromagnetics fundamentals
• RF circuit design
• High-frequency electronics
• Simulation tools (ADS, HFSS, CST)
