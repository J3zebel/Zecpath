Microwave Engineer

Job Overview
Works on high-frequency microwave systems used in radar, satellite, and communication
links.
Responsibilities
• Design microwave components (waveguides, filters, couplers)
• Develop radar and satellite communication systems
• Analyze signal propagation at microwave frequencies
• Test high-frequency circuits and antennas
• Ensure system efficiency and low signal loss
Skills Required
• Microwave theory and electromagnetics
• S-parameter analysis
• RF simulation tools
• High-frequency circuit design
