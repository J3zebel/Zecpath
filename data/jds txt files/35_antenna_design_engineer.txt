Antenna Design Engineer

Job Overview
Designs antennas used in wireless communication systems like mobile networks, satellites,
and IoT devices.
Responsibilities
• Design antennas for specific frequency ranges
• Simulate radiation patterns and gain
• Optimize antenna size, shape, and efficiency
• Test antenna performance in lab environments
• Work on 5G, satellite, and IoT antenna systems
Skills Required
• Electromagnetic field theory
• Antenna simulation tools (HFSS, CST)
• RF communication basics
• Signal radiation concepts
