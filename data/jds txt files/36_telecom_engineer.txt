Telecom Engineer

Job Overview
Works on designing, maintaining, and optimizing telecommunication networks and systems.
Responsibilities
• Install and maintain telecom infrastructure
• Optimize network performance and coverage
• Troubleshoot connectivity issues
• Work with mobile and broadband systems
• Ensure network reliability and uptime
Skills Required
• Telecom protocols (LTE, GSM, VoIP)
• Networking fundamentals
• Signal transmission knowledge
• Network troubleshooting
