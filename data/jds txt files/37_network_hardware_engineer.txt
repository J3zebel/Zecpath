Network Hardware Engineer

Job Overview
Designs and maintains hardware components used in communication networks such as
routers, switches, and base stations.
Responsibilities
• Design hardware architecture for networking devices
• Configure routers, switches, and communication modules
• Test hardware performance under load
• Troubleshoot network hardware failures
• Collaborate with software and telecom teams
Skills Required
• Networking hardware knowledge
• Digital electronics
• TCP/IP protocols
• Hardware debugging tools
