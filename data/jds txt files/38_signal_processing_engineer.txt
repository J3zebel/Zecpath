Signal Processing Engineer

Job Overview
Works on analyzing and processing signals to improve communication quality and data
transmission.
Responsibilities
• Design algorithms for signal filtering and enhancement
• Process audio, video, and communication signals
• Reduce noise and improve signal clarity
• Develop DSP (Digital Signal Processing) systems
• Work on real-time signal applications
Skills Required
• Digital Signal Processing (DSP)
• Fourier Transform, filters, wavelets
• MATLAB / Python
• Mathematics (linear algebra, probability)
