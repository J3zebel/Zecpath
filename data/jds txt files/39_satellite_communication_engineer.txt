Satellite Communication Engineer

Job Overview
Designs and manages satellite-based communication systems used for broadcasting,
navigation, and defense.
Responsibilities
• Design satellite communication links
• Work on uplink/downlink signal systems
• Maintain satellite ground stations
• Optimize transmission power and frequency usage
• Ensure reliable long-distance communication
Skills Required
• RF and microwave engineering
• Orbital mechanics basics
• Communication theory
• Satellite link budgeting
