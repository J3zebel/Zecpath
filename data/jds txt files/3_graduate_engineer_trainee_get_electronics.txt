Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET – Electronics)

Job Overview
A structured training program for fresh graduates to develop technical and industrial skills
before becoming full-time engineers.
Responsibilities
• Undergo training in electronics design and manufacturing
• Learn company products and systems
• Assist in production and R&D departments
• Work on mini-projects and assessments
• Rotate across departments (design, testing, production)
Skills Required
• Strong fundamentals in electronics engineering
• Problem-solving ability
• Basic programming knowledge (optional)
