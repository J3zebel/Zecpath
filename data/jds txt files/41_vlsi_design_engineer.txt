VLSI Design Engineer

Job Overview
Designs integrated circuits (ICs) used in electronic systems such as processors, memory
devices, and SoCs (System on Chip).
Key Responsibilities
• Design digital circuits using RTL coding (Verilog/VHDL)
• Develop system-level chip architecture
• Perform simulation and functional verification
• Optimize chip performance, power, and area (PPA)
• Collaborate with verification and physical design teams
Required Skills
• Verilog / VHDL
• Digital electronics fundamentals
• RTL design concepts
• EDA tools (Cadence, Synopsys)
