ASIC Design Engineer

Job Overview
Designs Application-Specific Integrated Circuits customized for specific applications (AI
chips, mobile processors, etc.).
Responsibilities
• Develop ASIC architecture and RTL design
• Work on low-power and high-performance chip design
• Optimize chip functionality for specific applications
• Perform design synthesis and simulation
• Ensure manufacturability and reliability
Skills Required
• Advanced digital design
• ASIC flow understanding
• Hardware description languages
• Timing and power optimization
