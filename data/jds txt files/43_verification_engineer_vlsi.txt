Verification Engineer (VLSI)

Job Overview
Ensures that chip designs function correctly before manufacturing.
Responsibilities
• Write test benches for RTL verification
• Perform functional and regression testing
• Identify and debug design errors
• Use simulation tools for verification
• Ensure design meets specifications
Skills Required
• SystemVerilog / Verilog
• UVM (Universal Verification Methodology)
• Simulation tools (ModelSim, VCS)
• Strong debugging skills
