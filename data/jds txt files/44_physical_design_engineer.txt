Physical Design Engineer

Job Overview
Converts logical chip design into physical layout ready for fabrication.
Responsibilities
• Perform floorplanning and placement
• Handle clock tree synthesis (CTS)
• Perform routing and timing closure
• Optimize chip layout for performance
• Ensure design meets manufacturing constraints
Skills Required
• VLSI physical design flow
• Timing analysis (STA)
• EDA tools (Innovus, ICC2)
• Semiconductor fabrication basics
