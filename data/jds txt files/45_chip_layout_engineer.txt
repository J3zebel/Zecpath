Chip Layout Engineer

Job Overview
Designs the physical arrangement of transistors and components inside a chip.
Responsibilities
• Create IC layout diagrams
• Ensure correct transistor placement
• Optimize silicon area usage
• Maintain design rule compliance (DRC)
• Work with fabrication guidelines
Skills Required
• CMOS design fundamentals
• Layout tools (Cadence Virtuoso)
• Semiconductor device physics
• Precision design skills
