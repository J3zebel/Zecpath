Power Electronics Engineer

Job Overview

Designs and develops systems that convert and control electrical power efficiently for
industrial, automotive, and renewable applications.
Key Responsibilities
• Design power conversion circuits (AC-DC, DC-DC, DC-AC)
• Develop switching power supplies and converters
• Work with power semiconductors (MOSFET, IGBT, SiC, GaN)
• Improve efficiency, thermal performance, and reliability
• Perform simulation and hardware testing
Required Skills
• Power electronics fundamentals
• Circuit simulation tools (PSIM, MATLAB, PLECS)
• Electrical machines basics
• Thermal and efficiency analysis
