PCB Design Trainee

Job Overview
Focused on learning printed circuit board design and layout creation for electronic devices.
Responsibilities
• Assist in PCB schematic creation
• Learn PCB layout design tools
• Place components and route circuits
• Support prototype PCB testing
• Ensure design follows industry standards
Skills Required
• PCB design software (Altium, KiCad, Eagle)
• Basic circuit theory
• Attention to detail
