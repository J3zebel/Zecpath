Battery Management System (BMS) Engineer

Job Overview
Designs systems that monitor and control battery packs in EVs and energy storage systems.
Responsibilities
• Monitor battery voltage, current, and temperature
• Design SOC (State of Charge) and SOH (State of Health) algorithms
• Ensure battery safety and thermal management
• Prevent overcharging and deep discharge
• Work with EV and storage systems
Skills Required
• Battery chemistry basics (Li-ion systems)
• Embedded systems knowledge
• Control algorithms
• Safety systems understanding
