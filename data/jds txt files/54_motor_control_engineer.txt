Motor Control Engineer

Job Overview
Develops control systems for electric motors used in EVs, robotics, and industrial systems.
Responsibilities
• Design motor control algorithms (FOC, PWM control)
• Optimize motor speed and torque
• Integrate sensors (Hall, encoder systems)
• Reduce power losses and heat generation
• Work with embedded controllers
Skills Required
• Control systems theory
• Electric machine fundamentals
• Embedded programming
• MATLAB/Simulink
