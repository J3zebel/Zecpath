Control Systems Engineer

Job Overview
Designs and implements control systems that regulate machines, processes, and industrial
systems automatically.
Key Responsibilities

• Design feedback control systems (PID, advanced controllers)
• Model and simulate dynamic systems
• Tune system performance for stability and accuracy
• Develop automation logic for industrial processes
• Work with sensors, actuators, and controllers
Required Skills
• Control theory (PID, state-space)
• MATLAB / Simulink
• Industrial automation basics
• System dynamics understanding
