PLC Programmer (Automation Engineer)

Job Overview
Programs Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) used in factories for machine automation.
Responsibilities
• Write PLC ladder logic programs

• Configure industrial machines and sensors
• Debug automation control systems
• Develop sequence control logic
• Interface PLC with SCADA systems
Skills Required
• PLC programming (Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Delta)
• Ladder logic, FBD, STL
• Industrial automation systems
• Electrical wiring knowledge
