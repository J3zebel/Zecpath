SCADA Engineer

Job Overview
Designs and maintains SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) systems for
monitoring industrial processes.
Responsibilities
• Develop SCADA dashboards for real-time monitoring
• Collect and analyze industrial data
• Integrate SCADA with PLC systems
• Monitor plant performance remotely
• Ensure system reliability and security
Skills Required
• SCADA software (Wonderware, WinCC, Ignition)
• Industrial networking (Modbus, OPC)
• Data acquisition systems
• Control systems basics
