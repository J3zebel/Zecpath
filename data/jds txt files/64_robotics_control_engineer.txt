Robotics Control Engineer

Job Overview
Develops control systems for robots used in manufacturing, healthcare, defense, and
automation industries.
Responsibilities
• Design robotic motion control systems
• Program robot path planning algorithms
• Integrate sensors for vision and navigation
• Work with robotic arms and autonomous systems
• Improve robot accuracy and stability
Skills Required
• Robotics kinematics and dynamics
• Control systems theory
• Embedded programming
• AI/vision integration basics
