Electronics Test Engineer

Job Overview
Ensures electronic products and systems function correctly by performing structured testing
at component and system levels.
Key Responsibilities

• Conduct functional testing of electronic circuits and devices
• Develop and execute test plans and test cases
• Identify hardware/software defects
• Use test equipment like oscilloscopes, multimeters, signal generators
• Document test results and failure reports
Required Skills
• Electronics fundamentals (analog + digital)
• Testing tools knowledge
• Circuit debugging
• Basic automation testing concepts
