Hardware Validation Engineer

Job Overview
Validates hardware designs to ensure they meet performance, safety, and design
specifications before mass production.
Responsibilities
• Validate prototype hardware systems
• Perform stress, load, and environmental testing
• Compare performance with design specifications
• Identify design weaknesses and improvement areas
• Collaborate with design engineers for fixes
Skills Required
• Hardware architecture understanding
• Signal analysis
• Testing methodologies
• Debugging tools (logic analyzers, oscilloscopes)
