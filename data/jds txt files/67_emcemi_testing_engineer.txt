EMC/EMI Testing Engineer

Job Overview
Ensures electronic devices do not emit or get affected by electromagnetic interference.
Responsibilities
• Perform EMC (Electromagnetic Compatibility) testing

• Measure EMI emissions and susceptibility
• Ensure compliance with international standards (CE, FCC, ISO)
• Design shielding and grounding solutions
• Prepare compliance certification reports
Skills Required
• Electromagnetic theory
• RF interference basics
• Testing standards knowledge
• Shielding techniques
