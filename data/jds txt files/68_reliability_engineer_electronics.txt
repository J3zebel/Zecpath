Reliability Engineer (Electronics)

Job Overview
Ensures electronic systems operate reliably over time under different environmental
conditions.
Responsibilities
• Perform life-cycle testing of electronic systems
• Analyze failure rates and degradation patterns
• Improve product durability and lifespan
• Conduct thermal, vibration, and stress testing
• Create reliability prediction reports
Skills Required
• Reliability engineering concepts
• Statistical analysis
• Failure modeling
• Electronics system knowledge
