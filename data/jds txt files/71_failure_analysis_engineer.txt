Failure Analysis Engineer

Job Overview
Investigates electronic product failures to identify root causes and prevent future issues.
Responsibilities
• Analyze failed electronic components
• Perform root cause analysis (RCA)
• Use microscopy and imaging tools for defect inspection

• Suggest design or process improvements
• Prepare failure analysis reports
Skills Required
• Semiconductor/electronics failure mechanisms
• Diagnostic tools usage
• Analytical thinking
• Materials and circuit understanding
