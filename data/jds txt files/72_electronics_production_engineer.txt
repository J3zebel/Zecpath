Electronics Production Engineer

Job Overview
Responsible for managing and improving the production of electronic products in
manufacturing environments.
Key Responsibilities
• Supervise electronic product assembly and production lines
• Ensure production targets and efficiency goals are met
• Troubleshoot production issues and downtime
• Coordinate with design, quality, and supply chain teams
• Monitor machine and operator performance
Required Skills
• Electronics manufacturing basics
• Production planning knowledge
• Problem-solving skills
• Industrial process understanding
