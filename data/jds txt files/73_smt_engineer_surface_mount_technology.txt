SMT Engineer (Surface Mount Technology)

Job Overview
Works on automated PCB assembly using SMT machines for placing electronic components
on boards.
Responsibilities
• Operate and maintain SMT machines (pick-and-place systems)

• Program SMT production lines
• Ensure correct component placement on PCBs
• Troubleshoot SMT defects (tombstoning, misalignment)
• Optimize soldering and reflow processes
Skills Required
• SMT process knowledge
• PCB assembly techniques
• Machine programming basics
• Soldering & defect analysis
