PCB Manufacturing Engineer

Job Overview
Focuses on manufacturing printed circuit boards used in electronic devices.
Responsibilities
• Oversee PCB fabrication processes (etching, layering, drilling)
• Ensure PCB quality and design compliance
• Work with chemical and mechanical processes
• Reduce defects in PCB production
• Coordinate with design and testing teams
Skills Required
• PCB fabrication knowledge
• Materials and chemical process basics
• Manufacturing standards
• Quality control understanding
