Lean Manufacturing Engineer (Electronics)

Job Overview
Focuses on improving efficiency by eliminating waste in electronics manufacturing
processes.
Responsibilities
• Implement lean manufacturing techniques (5S, Kaizen, Six Sigma)
• Reduce production waste and inefficiency
• Improve cycle time and productivity
• Analyze manufacturing processes
• Train staff on lean principles
Skills Required
• Lean manufacturing methodologies
• Data analysis and process optimization
• Quality improvement tools
• Industrial engineering basics
