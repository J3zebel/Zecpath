AI Hardware Engineer

Job Overview
Develops hardware systems optimized for artificial intelligence applications like machine
learning, computer vision, and edge AI.
Responsibilities
• Design AI-optimized hardware (GPUs, TPUs, NPUs)
• Optimize hardware for deep learning models
• Work on AI acceleration chips
• Collaborate with software ML teams
• Improve performance, power efficiency, and latency
Skills Required
• Digital design + VLSI basics
• Machine learning fundamentals
• Hardware acceleration concepts
• Parallel computing knowledge
