Cyber-Physical Systems Engineer

Job Overview
Designs systems where physical devices and software interact in real time (smart factories,
autonomous cars, robotics).
Responsibilities
• Integrate sensors, actuators, and software systems
• Develop real-time control systems
• Ensure safe interaction between physical and digital systems
• Work on automation and robotics applications
• Simulate system behavior
Skills Required
• Control systems
• Embedded programming
• Real-time systems
• System modeling and simulation
