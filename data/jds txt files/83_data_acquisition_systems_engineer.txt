Data Acquisition Systems Engineer

Job Overview
Develops systems that collect, process, and store real-world data from sensors and industrial
equipment.
Responsibilities
• Design sensor-based data collection systems
• Process analog and digital signals

• Convert physical signals into digital data
• Ensure accuracy and real-time data capture
• Integrate DAQ systems with software platforms
Skills Required
• Signal processing basics
• Sensor technology
• Embedded systems
• Data acquisition hardware/software
