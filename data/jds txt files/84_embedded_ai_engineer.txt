Embedded AI Engineer

Job Overview
Combines artificial intelligence with embedded systems to run AI models on edge devices
like cameras, robots, and smart sensors.
Responsibilities
• Deploy AI models on microcontrollers and edge devices
• Optimize machine learning models for low power systems
• Work on computer vision and speech recognition systems
• Integrate AI with embedded hardware
• Improve real-time inference performance
Skills Required
• Machine learning + deep learning
• Embedded C/C++
• TensorFlow Lite / PyTorch Mobile
• Edge AI frameworks
