Electronics Research Scientist

Job Overview
Works in research labs or R&D centers to develop new electronic technologies, systems, and
innovations.
Key Responsibilities

• Conduct advanced research in electronics, semiconductors, or communication systems
• Develop prototypes and experimental systems
• Publish research papers in journals and conferences
• Design innovative circuits, devices, or algorithms
• Collaborate with academic and industrial researchers
Required Skills
• Strong electronics fundamentals
• Research methodology
• Simulation tools (MATLAB, SPICE, HFSS, etc.)
• Analytical and problem-solving skills
