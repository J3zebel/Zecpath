DRDO Scientist (Electronics)

Job Overview
Works in the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) on advanced
defense electronics systems.

Responsibilities
• Develop defense-grade electronic systems
• Work on radar, missile guidance, and communication systems
• Design secure embedded and RF systems
• Conduct field testing and validation
• Collaborate with defense projects and missions
Skills Required
• Advanced electronics knowledge
• Signal processing and RF systems
• Embedded systems and control systems
• Security and defense technology understanding
