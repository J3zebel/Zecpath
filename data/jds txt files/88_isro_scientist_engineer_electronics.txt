ISRO Scientist Engineer (Electronics)

Job Overview
Works with the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) on satellite, launch vehicle, and
space communication systems.
Responsibilities
• Design satellite communication systems
• Develop onboard electronic systems for spacecraft
• Work on telemetry, tracking, and control systems
• Ensure reliability in space environments
• Support mission-critical electronics systems
Skills Required
• RF, communication, and satellite systems
• Embedded systems
• Control systems engineering
• High-reliability electronics design
