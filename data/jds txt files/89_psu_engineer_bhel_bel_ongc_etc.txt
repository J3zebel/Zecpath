PSU Engineer (BHEL, BEL, ONGC, etc.)

Job Overview
Works in Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) on large-scale industrial electronics and power
systems.

Responsibilities
• Maintain and design industrial electronic systems
• Work on power generation and control systems
• Support automation and instrumentation systems
• Ensure system safety and reliability
• Manage plant electronics operations
Skills Required
• Industrial electronics knowledge
• Power systems and control systems
• Instrumentation basics
• Electrical safety standards
