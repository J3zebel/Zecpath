R&D Engineer (Electronics Labs)

Job Overview
Works in corporate or industrial R&D labs to develop new electronic products and
technologies.
Responsibilities
• Design and develop new electronic prototypes
• Test and validate experimental systems
• Improve existing product designs
• Work on innovation and product development
• Collaborate with manufacturing and design teams
Skills Required
• Electronics design knowledge
• Circuit simulation and prototyping
• Embedded systems or VLSI basics
• Innovation and analytical thinking
