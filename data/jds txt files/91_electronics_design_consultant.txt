Electronics Design Consultant

Job Overview

Provides expert-level guidance in designing electronic circuits, systems, and products for
companies, startups, and individuals.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and review electronic circuit architectures
• Optimize product performance, cost, and reliability
• Solve complex circuit design problems
• Guide R&D teams in product development
• Conduct technical feasibility studies
Required Skills
• Strong analog and digital electronics knowledge
• Circuit simulation tools (SPICE, Multisim, LTspice)
• System-level design thinking
• Problem-solving expertise
