PCB Design Freelancer

Job Overview
Works independently on printed circuit board (PCB) design projects for clients across
industries.
Responsibilities
• Design PCB layouts from schematics
• Optimize routing, placement, and layer stack-up
• Ensure EMI/EMC compliance
• Generate Gerber files for manufacturing
• Collaborate with hardware engineers remotely
Skills Required
• PCB tools (Altium, KiCad, Eagle)
• Signal integrity basics
• Manufacturing design rules
• Attention to detail
