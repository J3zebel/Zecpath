Product Prototype Developer

Job Overview
Builds functional prototypes of electronic products for startups, inventors, and companies.
Responsibilities
• Convert ideas into working prototypes
• Design circuits and embedded systems
• Build and test hardware prototypes
• Iterate product design based on feedback
• Prepare prototype for manufacturing stage
Skills Required
• Circuit design and PCB development
• Embedded systems knowledge
• Rapid prototyping tools (Arduino, ESP32)
• Debugging and testing skills
