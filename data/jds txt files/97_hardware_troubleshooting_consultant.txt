Hardware Troubleshooting Consultant

Job Overview

Specializes in diagnosing and fixing complex electronic hardware failures for companies and
individuals.
Responsibilities
• Identify faults in electronic systems
• Repair and optimize hardware performance
• Analyze circuit failures and root causes
• Provide redesign recommendations
• Support field troubleshooting
Skills Required
• Strong electronics debugging skills
• Oscilloscope and test equipment usage
• Circuit analysis expertise
• Failure analysis knowledge
