AI Hardware Engineer

Job Overview
Designs specialized hardware that accelerates artificial intelligence workloads such as
machine learning, computer vision, and deep learning.
Key Responsibilities
• Design AI acceleration architectures (GPU/TPU/NPU systems)
• Optimize hardware for neural network performance
• Improve power efficiency and processing speed
• Collaborate with AI/ML software teams for model deployment
• Develop edge AI and data center hardware solutions
Required Skills
• Digital design & VLSI fundamentals
• Machine learning basics
• Computer architecture (parallel processing)
• Hardware optimization techniques
