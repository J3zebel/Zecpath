Quantum Electronics Engineer

Job Overview
Works on electronic systems used in quantum computing, quantum communication, and
quantum sensing technologies.
Responsibilities
• Design ultra-low noise control electronics for qubits
• Develop superconducting and cryogenic circuits
• Support quantum experiments and lab setups
• Improve signal stability and coherence times
• Work with quantum measurement systems
Skills Required
• Quantum physics fundamentals
• Advanced analog/digital electronics
• Cryogenic and low-noise circuit design
• Signal processing at extreme precision
